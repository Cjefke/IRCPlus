## 🥚 Eggdrop & TCL — Thousands of Scripts

The IRCPlus Eggdrop resource library contains **2,925 searchable resources**, covering multiple generations of Eggdrop scripting.

Whether you're maintaining a classic bot or building a modern IRC setup, the archive makes it easier to discover scripts, inspect their descriptions and find resources for your Eggdrop version.

**Browse the library:** [Eggdrop TCL Scripts](https://ircplus.nl/eggdrop-tcls.php)

### What's available?

- 🔎 Searchable TCL script archive.
- 🗂️ Scripts organised by Eggdrop version.
- 🧩 Channel management and moderation tools.
- 🎮 Games, trivia and entertainment scripts.
- 🔧 Utilities, logging and automation.
- 🌐 Network-related tools and integrations.
- 📚 Historical scripts and legacy resources.
- 🩹 Eggdrop patches and supporting documentation.

### Eggdrop source downloads

The download archive includes source releases from Eggdrop 0.7 through the 1.10 series, including historical versions for research and legacy compatibility.

- [Browse Eggdrop releases](https://ircplus.nl/eggdrop-download.php)
- [Explore Eggdrop patches and resources](https://ircplus.nl/downloads.php)

**Note:** Historical scripts may require modifications and may contain outdated assumptions. Check version compatibility and review the source before using scripts on a production bot.

### 🔌 Free ZNC Bouncer

Stay connected to IRC even when your computer or IRC client is offline.

ZNC acts as a persistent connection between your IRC client and network, allowing supported clients to reconnect to your bouncer.

- Connect using compatible IRC clients.
- Keep a persistent IRC session.
- Access your bouncer remotely.
- Use TLS-protected connections where configured.

**Learn more:** [Free ZNC Bouncer](https://ircplus.nl/free-bouncer.php)

Service availability, eligibility and account requirements depend on the applicable service rules.

## 🔒 Security & Responsible Use

IRCPlus includes both modern tools and historical software.

- Inspect scripts before loading them into your bots.
- Test legacy code in an isolated environment.
- Check compatibility with your Eggdrop and Tcl versions.
- Keep credentials and API keys out of source control.
- Use network testing tools only on systems you are authorised to test.
- Respect the licenses and attribution requirements of third-party resources.
